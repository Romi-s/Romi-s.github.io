01 — About

Bridging theory and working systems

I recently completed an MSc in Automation and Control Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, with work spanning hands-on robotics and end-to-end ML development — from deploying model predictive control on real robotic hardware to building deep-learning pipelines through deployment and inference.

My thesis combined these threads in an open-vocabulary perception system for robotic manipulation, using foundation models to produce structured scene graphs so a robot understands not only what it sees, but how objects relate in space. I'm currently extending it with ROS2 integration.

I enjoy turning research ideas into reliable systems and collaborating across disciplines to build automation and AI that actually runs.
